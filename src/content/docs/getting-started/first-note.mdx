---
title: Your first note
description: Add, save, edit and delete an invisible note in two minutes.
---

import { Steps } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

<Steps>

1. Open any file of your project and put the caret on a line you want to remember something about.

2. Press <kbd>Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Z</kbd> (<kbd>⌥⇧⌘Z</kbd> on macOS), or right-click the line and choose
   **Add Invisible Note**. A note field opens above the line.

3. Type your note. <kbd>Shift+Enter</kbd> starts a new line.

4. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to save it, or <kbd>Esc</kbd> to cancel.

</Steps>

The note now sits above the line. Look at the file on disk, or at your version control: nothing changed.

![Writing a note inline, above the line it belongs to](../../../assets/screenshots/3-write-a-note.png)

## Change or remove it

- **Edit:** double-click the note, change the text and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.
- **Delete:** right-click the note and choose **Delete Invisible Note**.
- **Changed your mind?** Press <kbd>Ctrl+Z</kbd> (<kbd>⌘Z</kbd>) and the deleted note comes back.
