Notes are stored on your computer, outside your project, in the folder JetBrains IDEs use for data they share:

<Tabs syncKey="os">
	<TabItem label="macOS">
		```
		~/Library/Application Support/JetBrains/InvisibleNotes/
		```
	</TabItem>
	<TabItem label="Windows">
		```
		%APPDATA%\JetBrains\InvisibleNotes\
		```
	</TabItem>
	<TabItem label="Linux">
		```
		~/.local/share/JetBrains/InvisibleNotes/
		```
	</TabItem>
</Tabs>

Each project has a folder in there, holding its notes in a `notes.json` file.

## What keeps your notes

- **Invalidate Caches, IDE updates and reinstalling the IDE** don't touch this folder.
- **Uninstalling the plugin** doesn't delete your notes.
- **Several JetBrains IDEs** open on the same project share its notes. If two of them save notes at the same time,
  both sets of changes are kept.

## Backing up

Copy the `InvisibleNotes` folder. To restore your notes, put it back while the IDE is closed.

## What notes aren't part of

Notes never go into your project's files, so they're never committed, never pushed and never shared with your team.
They're yours, on this computer.

<Aside type="caution" title="Moving the project folder">
	Notes belong to the project at its current location. If you move or rename the project's own folder, the IDE sees
	a new project and starts without notes. The old notes stay on disk, in their folder.
</Aside>
