Invisible Notes is a plugin for PhpStorm, IntelliJ IDEA, WebStorm, PyCharm and other JetBrains IDEs. It shows your
notes in the editor, between the lines of code, while the file itself stays untouched: there is nothing to commit
and nothing for version control to pick up. They work like comments that only you can see. Use them to record what
you worked out while reading unfamiliar code, to leave yourself reminders, or to keep questions for later. See
[what it's for](/invisible-notes/getting-started/what-its-for/), and when something else fits better.

## What it does

<CardGrid>
	<Card title="Notes in the editor" icon="pencil">
		Right-click a line and choose **Add Invisible Note**, or press <kbd>Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Z</kbd>
		(<kbd>⌥⇧⌘Z</kbd> on macOS). The note appears above the line, and the file doesn't change.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Notes follow the code" icon="code-branch">
		A note isn't tied to a line number. It stays with its line when you edit, reformat, pull changes or switch
		branches, and when files are renamed or moved.
	</Card>
	<Card title="See where the notes are" icon="magnifier">
		Each note has a mark next to the scrollbar, and the editor's top-right corner shows how many notes the file
		has.
	</Card>
	<Card title="There when you review your changes" icon="document">
		Notes also appear in [diff views](/invisible-notes/guides/diff-views/), such as the changes you're about to
		commit. They're only shown there, and they aren't part of the changes.
	</Card>
	<Card title="All notes in one place" icon="list-format">
		The Invisible Notes tool window lists the notes of the current file or of the whole project. Click one to
		jump to it.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Delete them all at once, and undo" icon="approve-check">
		Delete every note of a file, or of the whole project, in one step. You're asked first. Deleted a note by
		mistake? Undo brings it back, like any other edit. That goes for deleting them all, too.
	</Card>
	<Card title="Notes for AI tools" icon="puzzle">
		An AI tool connected to your IDE can explain or review code by adding notes next to it, without changing
		your files. See [Notes for AI tools](/invisible-notes/guides/ai-tools/).
	</Card>
	<Card title="Stored on your computer" icon="padlock">
		Notes are kept locally, outside the project, and the plugin doesn't send them anywhere. Clearing the IDE
		caches doesn't remove them, and they stay with a project whose folder you move or rename.
	</Card>
</CardGrid>

## Try it

Invisible Notes is sold through [JetBrains Marketplace](https://plugins.jetbrains.com/plugin/34596-invisible-notes), with a free 30-day trial. It works in PhpStorm
2025.2 and newer, and in other JetBrains IDEs from version 2025.2, such as IntelliJ IDEA, WebStorm, PyCharm, GoLand,
RubyMine, CLion, Rider and RustRover. In 2025.1 you get version 1.0.0, which has the notes but not what was added
since.
