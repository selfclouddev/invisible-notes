Invisible Notes is a paid plugin, sold through JetBrains Marketplace. JetBrains handles the checkout, the invoices
and the licenses, the same way as for JetBrains' own products.

## Free trial

You can try Invisible Notes free for **30 days**, with every feature. Start the trial right after
[installing the plugin](/invisible-notes/getting-started/installation/), when the IDE asks you to activate it. You
need a JetBrains Account for this.

## Prices

Invisible Notes is a subscription, paid monthly or yearly. The longer you stay subscribed without a break, the less
you pay: 20% off in the second year, and 40% off from the third year on.

### For individual use

For a license you buy and use yourself.

|             | First year | Second year | Third year onwards |
| ----------- | ---------- | ----------- | ------------------ |
| **Monthly** | $1.90      | $1.50       | $1.10              |
| **Yearly**  | $19.00     | $15.00      | $11.00             |

### For organizations

For licenses a company buys for its developers.

|             | First year | Second year | Third year onwards |
| ----------- | ---------- | ----------- | ------------------ |
| **Monthly** | $2.90      | $2.30       | $1.70              |
| **Yearly**  | $29.00     | $23.00      | $17.00             |

Prices are in US dollars, per user. JetBrains Marketplace shows them in your currency and adds taxes where they
apply.

### Keep using the version you paid for

A yearly subscription, or a monthly one paid for 12 months without a break, includes a **perpetual fallback
license**. If you stop subscribing, you can keep using the version that was current a year before your subscription
ended, forever. See the [license agreement](/invisible-notes/licensing/eula/#3-subscription).

### Discounts

Invisible Notes takes part in JetBrains' discount programs. Students, teachers and non-commercial open source
projects can get it for free, and universities, startups and non-profits at a discount. See the plugin's pricing
page on JetBrains Marketplace for the details.

## Activating a license

<Steps>

1. Buy a license on the plugin's JetBrains Marketplace page. It's linked to your JetBrains Account.

2. In the IDE, open **Help → Register**.

3. Select **Invisible Notes** and activate it with your JetBrains Account.

</Steps>

## When the trial or license ends

The IDE switches the plugin off until you activate a license. Your notes aren't deleted: they stay on your computer,
and appear again as soon as the plugin is active.
