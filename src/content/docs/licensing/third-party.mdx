## The plugin

Invisible Notes doesn't bundle any third-party libraries. It uses the libraries that come with the IDE, such as the
IntelliJ Platform and Gson.

Its license check is adapted from JetBrains' reference implementation,
[marketplace-makemecoffee-plugin](https://github.com/JetBrains/marketplace-makemecoffee-plugin), Copyright JetBrains
s.r.o., licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0).
