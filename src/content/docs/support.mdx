Questions, problems and ideas are all welcome: write to
[florin@selfcloud.ro](mailto:florin@selfcloud.ro).

## Reporting a problem

To help find the cause quickly, include:

- **Your IDE and its version:** copy it from **Help → About** (**PhpStorm → About PhpStorm** on macOS).
- **The Invisible Notes version:** in **Settings → Plugins → Installed**.
- **What you did, what you expected and what happened instead.** A screenshot helps.
- **Any error the IDE reported:** the red icon in the bottom-right corner opens the details.

You never need to send your notes. If a note's content matters for the problem, a description is enough.

## Licensing and payments

For questions about invoices, payments or license keys, you can also contact
[JetBrains Sales Support](https://sales.jetbrains.com/hc/en-gb), which handles purchases made through JetBrains
Marketplace.
